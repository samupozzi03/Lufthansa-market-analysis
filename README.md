# Lufthansa Financial Market Analysis (2019–2023 & Feb 2025 Focus)

## Project Overview
An empirical financial and econometric market study evaluating **Deutsche Lufthansa AG (LHAG.DE)** stock performance, return distribution, and price volatility from January 2019 to December 2023, along with a focused micro-case study on February 2025 events.

This analysis was developed as part of an academic project at the **University of Milan (Unimi)** using financial terminal data (LSEG Workspace) and quantitative modeling tools in Excel.

---

## 📊 Key Highlights & Quantitative Analysis
* **Aviation Sector & Business Model Analysis:** Overview of Lufthansa Group's integrated business structure, including passenger transport, Lufthansa Technik, Miles & More, and strategic M&A acquisitions (e.g., ITA Airways, Air Europa).
* **Price Evolution & Market Volatility:** Historical analysis of stock price dynamics vs. 20-day rolling volatility during major macroeconomic shocks (COVID-19 pandemic, Ukraine conflict, inflation/interest rate shifts).
* **Daily Logarithmic Returns Distribution:**
  * **Mean Daily Return:** -0.043%
  * **Median:** -0.029%
  * **Standard Deviation:** 2.810% (Variance: 0.078%)
  * **Kurtosis:** 4.727 (>3, exhibiting heavy tails and extreme market shock responses)
  * **Min / Max Log Returns:** -15.12% (March 12, 2020) / +18.12% (November 9, 2020)
* **Peer & Sector Benchmarking:** Indexed price performance comparison against direct European competitors (**Air France-KLM**, **Ryanair**, **EasyJet**) and the **STOXX® Europe Total Market Airlines Index (.T5751P)**.
* **Micro-Analysis (Feb 2025):** Impact assessment of strategic catalysts (completion of 41% ITA Airways acquisition, WestJet Technik agreement, EU antitrust approval).
